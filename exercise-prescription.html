Targeted Exercise

Exercise With A Purpose

Exercise prescription is about selecting the right movements, loading strategies and progression for the individual rather than simply following a generic workout.

Your programme can be structured around mobility, strength, stability, conditioning or performance depending on your current needs and objectives.

As your physical capacity changes, exercises and training demands can be progressively adapted to keep you moving forward.

Exercise Framework

Built Around Your Needs

A structured exercise approach that connects movement quality, physical capacity and the demands of your everyday life or sport.

01

Mobility

Develop usable movement range and mobility where it is relevant to your individual movement requirements.

02

Strength

Build the strength and force-producing capacity required for everyday activity, rehabilitation or performance.

03

Stability

Improve control and stability through progressive movement and loading strategies.

04

Core Control

Develop trunk strength and control to support efficient movement across different physical demands.

05

Conditioning

Build appropriate work capacity and conditioning according to your current fitness and activity requirements.

06

Performance

Progress exercise towards the specific physical qualities required for sport, training or demanding activities.
